Arduino Robotics Kit (Ages 6-13)

A beginner-friendly robotics kit with tutorials and hands-on projects. No coding experience required!

About Me

Hi! I'm Nayan! I'm a high school student who has always been fascinated by robotics from a young age. The problem was that everything online was too complicated for me. This is why I made this kit! I want to help kids learn and get interested in robotics with comprehensive tutorials and cool projects.

📅 Weekly Online Sessions

Every week, I host live online sessions where you can ask questions about your projects and get help in real-time. These sessions are designed to support your learning journey and make sure you feel confident every step of the way.
